How it works

From a tap on your ad to you standing in the yard

We run ads in your zip codes. When a homeowner fills out the form, they get a text right away and the lead comes to you. You call them, set a time, and go walk the job. Then you mark whether they were there. After your deposit, you only pay us for the ones where they were.

That is the whole loop. Below is each step, what you see, what the homeowner sees, and what we need from you to make it work.

1. Ads go live in your zip codes

We build the ads from real photos and videos of your work and run them under your Facebook page, in ad accounts in your business's name. You pay the ad platforms directly, on your own card. We ask for room to spend at least $50 a day, about $1,500 a month. Less than that and a month of ads doesn't produce enough leads to tell what is working.

The ads only show in the zip codes written down when you sign up. Those zip codes are yours: while you're in, we won't run ads for another tree company in them.

A homeowner who taps the ad fills out a short form with their job details and a cell number. The form is either built into Facebook, so they never leave the app, or on a page we set up. We pick whichever fits. You don't need a website for this, and your website, if you have one, stays as it is.

2. A text right away

The moment the form comes in, the homeowner gets an automatic text, so they know their request landed and that a call is coming. At the same time the lead comes to you, with what they put on the form, so you know who you're calling and what the job is.

Speed is the point of this step. A homeowner who just asked about a tree is still holding the phone. An hour later they may have asked two other companies. The text reaches them in that first moment, even while you're up a tree or running the chipper.

3. You call and set the time

You call the homeowner and set a time for the estimate. We don't call your leads. The sooner you call, the more of them show up, so call as soon as you can get down.

You set every time yourself, so you only book what your crews can get to. If you're booked out for weeks, email us and we pause the ads.

4. You go out and walk the job

You show up at the time you set, walk the job and write the quote, the same way you do now. Closing the job is yours. We don't quote for you and we don't take a cut of the work.

5. You mark each estimate, and only shown ones bill

Within 3 business days, you mark each estimate shown or not shown, the way we set up with you. An estimate counts as shown when a homeowner came in through your ads, you showed up at the time you set with them, and they were there to walk the job. If you don't mark one, it counts as shown.

These never bill: leads outside your zip codes, leads asking for work you don't do, homeowners who don't show, the same homeowner and job twice, and estimates you cancel. We bill once a week. If a charge looks wrong, email us within 14 days of the bill and we'll check the records and credit any that didn't count. The full pricing terms are here.

What we need from you

  • Real photos and videos of your work. Your own jobs beat stock photos, so the ads are built from them.
  • Access to your Facebook page. The ads run under your company's name.
  • A call to every lead, as soon as you can. We send the text. The call is yours.
  • A heads-up when things change. A new zip code, a service you stopped offering, a crew down for a week.
  • Showing up at the time you set. It is the one thing nobody can do for you, and the reason we turn away companies that can't.

How long it takes to start

Ads go live 1 to 2 weeks after the deposit is paid and we have your photos, your videos and access to your Facebook page. In that time we write the ads and set up the form and the text.

It starts with a trial, which is the first 30 days after your ads go live. If the trial doesn't bring at least one shown estimate, we refund the deposit in full. After that it's month to month, with no long-term contract. Email us and the ads go off within 2 business days.

Questions owners ask about the process

Who texts and who calls my leads?

We send every lead an automatic text the moment the form comes in. You make the call. We don't call your leads, so the homeowner talks to the company that will be doing the work.

How fast do I have to call?

As soon as you can. The text goes out right away, so the homeowner knows a call is coming, but the sooner you call, the more of them show up.

What if we get too busy?

You set every estimate time yourself, so you only book what your crews can get to. If you're booked out for weeks, email us and we'll pause the ads.

Do I need a website for this to work?

No. The ads use a short form, either one built into Facebook or a page we set up, and we pick whichever fits. Your website, if you have one, stays as it is.

How do I mark an estimate shown or not shown?

The way we set up with you when you start, within 3 business days of the visit. An estimate you don't mark counts as shown, so marking the no-shows is what keeps your bill right.
