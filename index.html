The deal

You pay for estimates that happen

Per-estimate ads 7 terms

  1. Ad spend

    You pay the ad platform directly, on your own card. That money never passes through us.

  2. Setup

    You pay us a one-time setup fee. That covers your landing page and the text, call, booking, and reminders for every lead.

  3. Per estimate

    After setup, you pay a flat fee for each shown estimate.

  4. What counts as shown

    A homeowner booked through your ads, you showed up at the booked time, and they were there to walk the job.

  5. What does not bill

    Leads outside your service area. Leads asking for work you don't do. Homeowners who don't show.

  6. Your area is yours

    We take one tree company per service area. Once you're in, we won't run ads for another tree company there.

  7. Term

    There is no long-term contract. You can stop whenever you want.

The setup fee and the per-estimate fee are set on the call, based on your area and your mix of jobs.

FAQ

Straight answers before the call

I got burned by an agency before.

Most agencies charge a monthly retainer whether your phone rings or not. After setup, we only get paid when a homeowner is standing in the yard waiting for your estimate.

Facebook leads are tire kickers.

A lot of them are. That's why you don't pay us per lead. The ones who fill out a form and disappear still cost ad spend, so every lead hears from you inside a minute, before they forget they asked.

Who pays for the ads?

You do, straight to the ad platform. Plan on at least [$___] a month. You can see every dollar in the ad account.

What counts as shown?

You got to the property at the booked time and the homeowner was there to walk the job. If they weren't, you don't pay for it.

Is there a contract?

Not a long-term one. You agree to the setup fee and the per-estimate fee. If you want to stop, tell us and we turn the ads off.

How fast does it start?

Ads go live about [___ days] after setup is paid and we have access to your calendar.

Do I need a website?

No. The ads send homeowners to a landing page we build for you. If you already have a website, it stays as it is.
