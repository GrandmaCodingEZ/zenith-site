The deal

You pay for estimates that happen

How it compares

Retainer agency
You pay the agency every month, on top of ad spend, whether your phone rings or not.
Shared lead site
You pay per lead, and other companies can buy the same lead.
Zenith Co.
You pay ad spend on your own card and a one-time setup fee. Our only other fee is for estimates the homeowner was there for.

Typical setups. Some agencies and lead sites work differently.

Per-estimate ads 9 terms

  1. Ad spend

    You pay the ad platforms directly, on your own card. Plan on at least $50 a day, about $1,500 a month. The ad accounts are in your business's name, and you keep them if you stop.

  2. Setup

    You pay us a one-time setup fee. That covers your landing page and the text, call, booking, and reminders for every lead.

  3. Per estimate

    After setup, you pay a flat fee for each shown estimate. We bill once a week.

  4. What counts as shown

    A homeowner booked through your ads, you showed up at the booked time, and they were there to walk the job.

  5. What does not bill

    Leads outside your service area. Leads asking for work you don't do. Homeowners who don't show. Duplicate bookings. Estimates you cancel.

  6. You mark each one

    Within 3 business days, mark each estimate shown or not shown, the way we set up with you. If you don't mark it, it counts as shown. If a charge looks wrong, email us within 14 days of the bill and we'll check the records and credit any that didn't count.

  7. Your area is yours

    Your area is written down when you sign up. While you're in, we won't run ads for another tree company there.

  8. What we need from you

    Give us access to your ad accounts and calendar. Keep the open times on your calendar current. Tell us when your service area or the work you take changes.

  9. Term

    There is no long-term contract. Email us and the ads go off within 2 business days. The landing page we host comes down with them.

The setup fee and the per-estimate fee are set on the call, based on your area and your mix of jobs. Every term, explained

FAQ

Straight answers before the call

Facebook leads are tire kickers.

A lot of them are. That's why you don't pay us per lead. The ones who fill out a form and vanish still cost ad spend, so we text and call each one right away, before they forget they asked.

What if we get too busy?

Leads can only book times that are open on your calendar, so block off the days you're full. If you're booked out for weeks, email us and we'll pause the ads.

How fast does it start?

Ads go live 1 to 2 weeks after setup is paid and we have access.

Do I need a website?

No. The ads send homeowners to a landing page we build for you. Your website stays as it is.

Is my area still open?

Put your counties in the form when you book. We'll tell you on the call if another tree company already has them.
