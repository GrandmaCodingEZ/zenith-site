You pay for estimates that happen

The setup fee and the per-estimate fee are set on the call, based on your area and your mix of jobs.

  1. Ad spend

    You pay the ad platform directly, on your own card. That money never passes through us.

  2. Setup

    You pay us a one-time setup fee. That covers your landing page and the text, call, booking, and reminders for every lead.

  3. Per estimate

    After setup, you pay a flat fee for each shown estimate.

  4. What counts as shown

    A homeowner booked through your ads, you showed up at the booked time, and they were there to walk the job.

  5. What does not bill

    Leads outside your service area. Leads asking for work you don't do. Homeowners who don't show.

  6. Your area is yours

    We take one tree company per service area. Once you're in, we won't run ads for another tree company there.

  7. Term

    There is no long-term contract. You can stop whenever you want.
