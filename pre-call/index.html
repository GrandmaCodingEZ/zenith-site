Before we talk

Get your questions answered

These are the ones owners ask first. Bring the rest to the call.

What counts as a shown appointment?

A homeowner in your service area meets you at the property for the estimate. That's the only time you pay us. If they don't show, cancel, or turn out to be outside your service area, you don't pay. You mark each visit shown or not shown within 3 business days. Whether the job closes is up to you.

How fast do leads come in?

Once your ads are live, leads usually start coming in within 1 to 2 weeks. Every lead gets a text from us right away, and the faster you call them back, the more of them show up. On the call I'll tell you what to expect for your zip codes.

Who pays for the ad spend?

You do. It goes straight from your account to Facebook, not through us, so you see every dollar. We usually recommend around $50 a day. Our fee is separate: a $500 deposit to start, then $197 for each shown appointment.

Is there a long-term contract?

No. It's month to month. We start with a trial, so you see leads coming in before you commit to anything, and I'll go over how the trial works on the call. If it isn't working for you, you aren't locked in.

What if the ads don't work?

You only pay us the $197 when a homeowner shows up for the estimate. If nobody shows, you pay us nothing per appointment. What you put in is the $500 deposit and your ad spend, which goes straight to Facebook. If the trial doesn't bring at least one shown appointment in your first month, we refund the full deposit. We check the numbers with you during the trial, so you never have to guess whether it's working.

How is this different from Angi or Thumbtack?

On those sites you pay for a lead that usually goes to other companies too, so you're racing them to call back, and you pay whether the homeowner answers or not. With us the ads run for your business only, and we don't take another tree company in your zip codes. The homeowner is asking for you, not a list. And you pay per shown appointment, not per lead.

Do I need a website to run ads?

No. The ads can run on a form built into Facebook, so homeowners never have to leave the app. A lot of them will look you up on Google before the estimate, though, and a good website and good reviews make them more likely to show up. If you don't have those yet, we can help with both, but you don't need them to start.

What do I have to do on my end?

Three things. Send us real photos and videos of your work, because real jobs beat stock photos every time. Give us access to your Facebook page so we can run the ads under your name. And when a lead comes in, call them as soon as you can. We handle the leads, the form and the follow-up text. You handle the closing.

Do you work with other tree companies in my area?

Not in your zip codes. Each client gets their own zip codes, and once they're taken, we don't add another tree company there. If I ran your ads and a competitor's down the road, you'd be bidding against each other, which is the opposite of what I want. I check that your zip codes are open before I run any ads.

How do you make sure leads are qualified?

The ads only show to homeowners in your zip codes, and each lead fills out a short form. They get a text back right away, and the sooner you call, the more of them show up. A lead outside your service area never bills, and neither does a homeowner who isn't there.

How do I see what's happening?

Your ad spend goes straight from your account to Facebook, so you see every dollar. Every lead comes to you with what the homeowner put on the form, so you know who you're calling. And during the trial we go over the numbers with you, so you never have to guess whether it's working.
