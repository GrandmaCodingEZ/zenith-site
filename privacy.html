Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 1, 2026 · Last updated: August 1, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Zenith Co. ("Zenith," "we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and discloses information when you visit zenithcomarketing.com (the "Site"), respond to our advertising, contact us, book a call, or use our products and services (the "Services"). It also describes your privacy rights and how to exercise them. By using the Site or Services, you agree to the practices described in this Policy.

Questions? Contact us at hello@zenithcomarketing.com.

Quick summary

· We collect contact, business, billing, and online activity information.

· We use it to provide our Services, respond to inquiries, market to prospective clients, and run our business.

· We share it with service providers (such as our CRM and payment processor) — we do not sell personal information for money.

· Calls with us may be recorded, with notice at the start of the call.

· You can request access, correction, or deletion at any time — see "Your privacy rights."

1. Text messaging (SMS) policy

When you provide your mobile number — for example when booking a call, requesting an audit, or becoming a client — you consent to receive SMS messages from us related to your inquiry and our Services, including notifications, account communications, and (only if you opt in) marketing messages.

  • Opt out: reply STOP to any message at any time.
  • Help: reply HELP for assistance.
  • Frequency: message frequency varies based on your interactions with us.
  • Cost: standard message and data rates apply per your carrier's terms.

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties, except that phone numbers may be processed by service providers (such as our SMS platform and CRM, GoHighLevel/LeadConnector) solely to deliver the messages you have consented to receive.

3. Where we get information

  • Directly from you — when you email us, book a call, fill out a form, or become a client.
  • From your device — through our hosting provider's standard logs and, if we adopt them, cookies or analytics tools (see Section 6). The Site also loads fonts from Google Fonts, which involves your browser sending a request (including your IP address) to Google's servers.
  • From service providers — including our CRM and booking platform (GoHighLevel/LeadConnector) and our payment processor.
  • From advertising platforms — if you respond to an ad we run (such as Google or Meta ads).
  • From publicly available sources — business directories, public records, and similar.

4. How we use information

  • Provide and operate the Services — onboarding, building and hosting your website, configuring automations, billing, and support.
  • Sales and marketing — respond to inquiries, follow up on leads, and send promotional email or SMS where consented.
  • Analytics and improvement — understand how the Site is used and improve our offering.
  • Advertising measurement — measure the performance of any advertising campaigns we run.
  • Quality assurance and training — review call recordings to improve our service.
  • Security, fraud prevention, and legal compliance — protect the Site and Services and comply with applicable law.

6. Cookies and tracking

The Site does not currently set its own cookies or run session-replay technology. Our hosting infrastructure collects standard access logs. If we adopt analytics or advertising tools that use cookies or pixels, we will update this Policy. Most browsers let you block or delete cookies through their settings, and industry opt-outs are available at optout.aboutads.info.

7. Call recording

Sales, onboarding, and support calls with us may be recorded for quality assurance, training, recordkeeping, and dispute resolution. Where calls are recorded, we provide notice at the start of the call; by continuing after that notice, you consent to the recording. If you do not wish to be recorded, tell us at the start of the call and we will end or pause the recording. Recordings are accessible only to authorized personnel and our service providers.

9. Your privacy rights

Depending on the state where you live, you may have some or all of the following rights: to know/access the personal information we hold about you; to delete it; to correct it; to receive it in a portable format; to opt out of sale, sharing, or targeted advertising; to limit use of sensitive personal information; to non-discrimination for exercising your rights; and, in certain states, to appeal a denied request.

To exercise any right, email hello@zenithcomarketing.com with your request. We will acknowledge it promptly, verify your identity by confirming details we already have on file, and respond within 45 days (with a further 45-day extension where permitted, in which case we will notify you). Your first request in any 12-month period is free. You may designate an authorized agent with written, signed authorization. If we deny your request and your state provides an appeal right, you may appeal by replying to our decision with the subject line "Appeal."

10. Children's privacy

The Site and Services are business-to-business and are not directed to children under 16. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe we have, contact us and we will delete it.

11. Data security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to protect personal information, including encryption in transit and access controls. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

12. International visitors

The Site is operated from the United States. If you access it from outside the U.S., your information will be transferred to and processed in the United States.

13. Third-party links

The Site links to third-party websites (including client websites we showcase and our booking calendar). This Policy does not apply to those third parties; we encourage you to read their privacy policies.

14. Changes to this policy
