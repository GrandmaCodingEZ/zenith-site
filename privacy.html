Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 1, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Zenith Co. ("we," "us," or "our") handles information in connection with the website zenithcomarketing.com (the "Site"). We keep this simple because our data practices are simple: we collect as little as possible.

Information you provide to us

The Site does not currently include forms or account features. If you contact us — for example by emailing hello@zenithcomarketing.com or booking a call — we receive the information you choose to share, such as your name, email address, phone number, business name, and the contents of your message. We use it to respond to you, provide our services, and manage our business relationship with you.

Information collected automatically

Like most websites, the Site's hosting infrastructure may log standard technical information such as IP address, browser type, pages visited, and timestamps. The Site is hosted on GitHub Pages, operated by GitHub, Inc.; GitHub may collect visitor logs as described in its own privacy documentation. The Site also loads fonts from Google Fonts, which involves your browser sending a request (including your IP address) to Google's servers.

We may in the future use analytics tools (such as Google Analytics) or advertising platforms (such as Google Ads or Meta) to understand Site traffic and measure campaigns. These tools may use cookies or similar technologies. If we adopt them, we will update this policy.

Text messaging (SMS)

If you provide us your phone number — for example when requesting an audit, quote, or callback — you consent to receive text messages from us related to your inquiry and our services. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. You can opt out at any time by replying STOP, or get assistance by replying HELP.

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Information sharing with subcontractors in support services, such as customer service, is permitted. All other use categories exclude text messaging originator opt-in data and consent; this information will not be shared with any third parties.

How we share information

We do not sell your personal information. We share information only with:

  • Service providers that help us operate — such as hosting, email, scheduling, and marketing-automation platforms — and only as needed to provide our services.
  • Legal requirements — if required by law, regulation, or valid legal process.

Data retention

We keep correspondence and business records only as long as needed for the purposes described above or as required by law, then delete them.

Your choices and rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information we hold about you by emailing hello@zenithcomarketing.com. We will respond to reasonable requests within a reasonable time.

Children

The Site is a business-to-business service and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The effective date above reflects the latest revision. Material changes will be posted on this page.

Contact

Questions about this policy: hello@zenithcomarketing.com
