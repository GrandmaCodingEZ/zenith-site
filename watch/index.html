FAQ

Straight answers before the call

Who is this for?

Established tree companies in Florida with two or more crews ready for more work and room for at least $50 a day in ad spend. It isn't for new or owner-only businesses, or for anyone who can't show up at the booked time.

Facebook leads are tire kickers.

A lot of them are. That's why you don't pay us per lead. The ones who fill out a form and vanish still cost ad spend, so we text and call each one right away, before they forget they asked.

What if a homeowner books and isn't there?

You mark it not shown within 3 business days and it doesn't bill. The same goes for leads outside your area, work you don't do, duplicate bookings, and estimates you cancel.

How fast does it start?

Ads go live 1 to 2 weeks after setup is paid and we have access.

Is my area still open?

Put your counties in the form when you book. We'll tell you on the call if another tree company already has them.

Keep your crews booked.

If your crews can take on more estimates, answer four questions and pick a time to talk.
