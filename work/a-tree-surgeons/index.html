Our work · Tampa, FL

A Tree Surgeons Enterprise

A Tree Surgeons Enterprise is Fred's tree company in Tampa, and it was our first client. We built the website with the estimate form on the first screen, set up a text back for every missed call, and a review request that goes out after every job.

atreesurgeons.net
A Tree Surgeon's Enterprise
Tampa's Tree Surgeons Since Day One Honest Tree Work. Every Time.

The problem it solved

In a tree company the owner is usually the most skilled person on the job, which means the owner is the one up the tree when the phone rings. Calls that ring out go to voicemail, and a homeowner with a limb on the roof doesn't leave a message and wait. They call the next company.

The other leak was people who looked the company up and found nowhere to ask for an estimate without calling.

What we built

  • Estimate form on the first screen. Name, phone and the service needed, right at the top, so a homeowner can ask without calling. The form tells them the company will text right back.
  • A text back for every missed call. When a call rings out during a job, the caller gets a text from the company right away instead of a voicemail greeting.
  • Review requests after every job. When a job is done, the customer gets a request for a Google review while the yard still looks the way the crew left it.
  • A page for each service and the Tampa-area towns it covers. Removals, trimming, stump grinding, palm work, storm cleanup and emergency calls, and the towns from Brandon to Wesley Chapel.

What it taught us

Every one of these fixes is about the minutes after a homeowner reaches out. A text back in seconds keeps a missed call alive. That is the same idea our per-estimate ads service is built on: whoever answers first usually gets the visit, so every lead gets a text and a call in your company's name, aiming for under a minute. How that works.

The site is live at atreesurgeons.net. Open it on your phone, on cell data, the way a homeowner would.
