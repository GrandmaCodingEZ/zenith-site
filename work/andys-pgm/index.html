Our work · Sarasota, FL

Andy's PGM

Andy's PGM (Professional Grounds Maintenance) is Andy's tree, landscape and grounds company in Sarasota, in business since September 1988. We built its first website: a page for every service and town, a photo estimate form, and a gallery of the crew's own work.

andyspgm.com
Andy's PGM
Sarasota, FL · Since 1988 No job too tall or too small. One call does it all.

The problem it solved

Andy's PGM has done tree work in Sarasota since 1988, with a crane and a bucket truck, and had no website of its own. What a homeowner found when they searched was a directory listing that hadn't been touched since about 2016: hours and a phone number, no photos, and no way to ask for an estimate. A company that established should be the obvious choice in its own town, and it can't be if nobody can see the work.

What we built

  • A page for every service. Tree removal, crane removal, trimming and pruning, palm trimming, stump grinding, storm cleanup, landscape and palm installation, sod, and grading, each with photos from the crew's own jobs. A separate page covers the grounds-maintenance side for condo and commercial properties.
  • A photo estimate form. A homeowner fills in their name, phone, the job address and what they need, and can attach a photo of the tree. The request goes to Andy.
  • Call first on phones. The number sits on the first screen and in a bar at the bottom of every page on a phone, one tap away.
  • Their own photos and videos, no stock. Every picture on the site is from an Andy's PGM job, in a gallery and spread across the service pages.
  • Pages for the towns they cover. Sarasota, Bradenton and Charlotte County, plus seven plain-language articles on the things homeowners ask, like when to trim before hurricane season and how a big tree comes down.

What it taught us

A company that has been around since 1988 doesn't need to convince anyone it can do the work. It needs the homeowner who just searched to find it, see the work, and reach it in one tap. The website handles that. Our per-estimate ads service handles what comes next: being in front of the homeowner at the moment they search, then texting and calling every lead in the company's name, aiming for under a minute. How that works.

The site is live at andyspgm.com. Open it on your phone, on cell data, the way a homeowner would.

About this build

What did Zenith Co. build for Andy's PGM?

The website, andyspgm.com: a page for each service and town, a grounds-maintenance page, a photo estimate form, a gallery of the company's own photos and videos, and articles for homeowners. We host it.

Are the photos on andyspgm.com real?

Yes. Every photo and video on the site is from an Andy's PGM job. There are no stock photos.

Is Andy's PGM on the pay-per-shown-estimate service?

No. Our work for Andy's PGM is the website. The per-estimate ads service is separate, and it takes one tree company per service area.
