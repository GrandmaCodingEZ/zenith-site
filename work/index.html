Why there are no lead or revenue numbers here

We only publish numbers we can stand behind. A month of tree work moves with the weather, the season and a dozen things that have nothing to do with marketing, so a "leads up 300%" claim usually says more about the agency than the client. The live sites are proof you can check in ten seconds.
